      # Privacy Policy


      ## Responsible Party


      The responsible party within the meaning of data protection laws (in particular the GDPR) is:


      Pfingstgemeinde Bethanien e.V.  
      Member of the Bund Freikirchlicher Pfingstgemeinden KdöR  
      Darius Kletter  
      Nürnberger Straße 24  
      85055 Ingolstadt, Germany  
      Phone: 0841 920887  
      Email: [hello@betania.de](mailto:hello@betania.de)  


      ## General Notes


      We take the protection of your personal data very seriously. Personal data
      is treated by us confidentially and in accordance with the statutory data
      protection regulations as well as this privacy policy.


      The processing of your data is carried out exclusively for the purpose of
      establishing contact and processing your requests.


      ## Contact Form and Email


      When you contact us by email or via the contact form, we process your first
      and last name, your email address, optionally your phone number, the reason
      you selected (e.g. "Faith in Jesus" or "Baptism") and your message. We use
      this data exclusively to process your request and for any follow-up
      questions. The form is delivered to us by email through our email provider
      (one.com).


      The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give by
      ticking the checkbox in the form. If your details reveal religious beliefs,
      processing is based on your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). For
      requests sent by email, the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR. We do
      not pass your data on to third parties.


      ## Storage and Deletion of Data


      We adhere to the principles of data minimisation and data economy.  
      We store personal data only for as long as is necessary to achieve the
      stated purposes or as required by statutory retention periods.

      After the respective purpose ceases to apply or the statutory periods
      expire, the data is routinely deleted or blocked.


      ## Hosting and Reach Measurement


      This website is hosted by Vercel Inc. (USA). When you visit the site,
      technically necessary data (e.g. IP address, date and time, page visited,
      browser type) is processed in server log files. We also use Vercel Web
      Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights to evaluate page views and load times
      without cookies and without building user profiles.


      The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running the website securely,
      reliably and quickly (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Where data is transferred to the
      USA, this is based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data
      Privacy Framework.


      ## Event Calendar (ChurchTools)


      An event calendar from ChurchTools is embedded on the events page. When you
      open that page, your IP address is transmitted to ChurchTools' servers so
      that the calendar can be displayed.


      ## Links to Google Maps


      Our website links to Google Maps (directions to our church building). No
      map is embedded; data is only transmitted to Google once you click the
      link. For information on data processing by Google, please refer to
      [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).


      ## Your Rights


      You have the right at any time to:


      - **Information** about your personal data stored with us,
      - **Correction** of inaccurate data,
      - **Restriction of processing** (blocking),
      - **Deletion** of your data (provided no statutory retention obligation exists),
      - **Objection** to the processing of your data,
      - as well as the **withdrawal of any consent given** with effect for the future.


      Please contact the responsible party mentioned above.


      You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection
      supervisory authority. The competent authority is the Bavarian State Office
      for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach.


      If there is a statutory obligation to archive data, the processing of your
      data will be restricted (blocked) rather than deleted.


      ## Changes to this Privacy Policy


      We reserve the right to occasionally update this privacy policy so that it
      always complies with current legal requirements or to implement changes to
      our services (e.g. when introducing new services).


      The updated privacy policy will apply to your next visit.
