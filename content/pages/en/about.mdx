      ## Jesus is the center of our church

      We are a **young and modern church**. A church that is full of
      **fire** and **love** for the Lord. Our heart beats for Jesus!

      Jesus Christ died for our sins and rose from the dead on the third day!
      (1 Cor 15:3-4)

      Hallelujah, we have peace with God – what a joy!

      ## Our Mission

      To spread the good news in Ingolstadt and throughout the whole world!

      ## Our History

      Our story begins in the German church [City Church](https://www.citychurchingolstadt.de/) in Ingolstadt.
      Here we started a home group that, after a short time, grew into a
      strong community. Normally a home group has between five and twelve
      people, but ours quickly grew to 35 people plus many children!

      In 2001 we decided to found our own church. The Pentecostal
      Church Bethanien e.V. was established within the [BFP](https://bfp.de/) (Bund Freikirchlicher
      Pfingstgemeinden).

      Then in October 2007 we finally had our own church building at
      Nürnberger Straße. Since then we have been praising and worshipping the
      Lord at [Nürnberger Str. 24](https://maps.app.goo.gl/w3A3ZUWggsQTQzJb7).

      Today we are a large, vibrant church/family with over 140 members
      who are passionate about sharing the Gospel and above all making Jesus known.
